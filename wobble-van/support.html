Wobble Van – Support

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Wobble Van is a cosy puzzle game about packing a moving van. Stuck, found a bug, or missing a purchase? We are happy to help.

Contact

E-mail: jibbgamestudio@gmail.com
Please tell us your phone model and the level you are on. We usually answer within a few days.

You can also send us a note from inside the game: tap the cog on the map, then the speech bubble.

A purchase is missing

Open the shop on the map, go to the Coins tab and tap Restore Purchases. The starter pack comes back on any phone signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Coin packs are used up when they are given, so they cannot be restored – if coins you paid for never arrived, write to us.

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.
