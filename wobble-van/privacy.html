Wobble Van – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 (iPhone: App Store purchases and Apple's tracking question)

Wobble Van is a puzzle game about packing a moving van, for Android phones (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store). This page explains what happens with information when you play it. Where the two differ, it says so.

What we collect

We, the makers of Wobble Van, do not sell any information. The game has no accounts of its own, and we run no servers that receive data about you. The only things that reach us are crash reports, and notes you choose to send – with your e-mail address only if you choose to add it (see below).

Your progress

Your progress – levels won, stars, coins, the furniture album and your settings – is saved in a small file on your device.

On Android: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of that file is also kept in your Google account (Saved Games), so your progress follows you to a new phone. Android's own backup may also keep a copy in your Google account. Neither copy is sent to us. You can delete the saved game in the Google Play Games app or your Google account; uninstalling the game deletes the copy on the phone.

On iPhone: the game does not use iCloud or Game Center yet, so your progress stays only on your iPhone and is not copied to any account by the game. (Your iPhone's own device backup may include it, under Apple's terms.) Deleting the game deletes your progress. A starter pack you bought comes back on a new iPhone through Apple – see "Purchases" below.

Crash reports and feedback (Sentry)

When the game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix bugs. A report holds technical details only: what went wrong in the game's code, the game version, how far you have got, and your device model and system version. It does not hold your name, contacts or location, and we turn off sending your IP address as personal data. If you write to us with the speech bubble in the settings, your note is sent the same way together with the game version and your level. Please don't write personal details in it.

If you would like an answer, you can also type your e-mail address in the box under your note. This is optional: leave it empty and no address is sent. If you give one, it is sent to Sentry with your note and we use it only to reply to you about that note – never for marketing, and it is never sold or shared. Reports and notes, including any address, are kept for up to 90 days and are used only to fix the game and answer you. To have your note and address deleted sooner, write to the contact address below.

Optional video ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward, for example three more moves. Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with the ad. Only ads suitable for all ages are requested.

On iPhone – Apple's tracking question (App Tracking Transparency): before ads can use your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA), the game shows Apple's own question asking whether you allow tracking, together with a short explanation from Google's consent tool. If you tap Allow, Google AdMob may use the IDFA to show and measure ads that suit you better. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared (Apple gives the game only zeros) and ads are not personalised with it; the game works exactly the same either way. You can change this at any time in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. We, the makers, never see or keep the IDFA.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the game's settings (the cog on the map). Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

You can buy packs of coins and a starter pack. We never see your card, Apple ID or payment details, and we keep no record of your purchases on any server.

On Android: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. Google Play tells the game only which pack was bought, so the coins can be given to you.

On iPhone: payments are handled entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple tells the game only which pack was bought and a transaction number, so the pack can be given to you once; the game keeps those numbers in its save file on your iPhone so a purchase is never given twice. The starter pack is bought once and can be restored on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID: it comes back by itself, or tap Restore Purchases in the shop's Coins tab. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Permissions

The game uses the internet only for ads, purchases, crash reports and your feedback, and vibration for a short buzz in play. On Android, Google's ads and billing libraries add the permissions they need for this (network state, advertising ID). On iPhone, the only question the game asks is Apple's tracking question described above. The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is made for everyone. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Ads are limited to those suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change reaches the game.
