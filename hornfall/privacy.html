Hornfall Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Hornfall is a tower defense game for Android and iOS made by Jibb Games. This page explains, in plain words, what information is handled when you play it, who handles it and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Jibb Games is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: jibbgamestudio@gmail.com.

In short

  • The game has no accounts, no login and no cloud save. Your progress stays on your device.
  • We do not use analytics tools and we do not sell any information.
  • Ads are only shown when you tap to watch one. They come from Google AdMob.
  • Crash reports, and feedback notes you choose to send, go to Sentry so we can fix the game.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play or Apple. We never see your payment details.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels won, stars, upgrades, commander level, Daily streak, bestiary, achievements, tutorial state) and your settings (music and sound volume) are saved in a small file on your device. It is not sent to us. Your phone's own backup (Android Auto Backup or iCloud device backup) may keep a copy in your Google or Apple account under their terms. Uninstalling the game deletes the file on the device.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward: extra gold before a wave (at most twice per level) or a revive with 5 extra lives (once per level). Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one, and never in Daily or Last Stand. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, identifiers such as the advertising ID on Android, and how you interact with the ad. We ask AdMob only for ads suitable for a general audience.

How Google uses this information is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent and how to change it

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads can be used. You can say no; the game works the same and only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings.

iPhone and iPad

On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track you and does not use your device's advertising identifier. Only non-personalised ads are requested. Google may still collect the technical information described above to show and measure those ads.

Purchases

You can buy Hornfall's Patron, a one-time purchase that gives the ad rewards without watching ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (on Android) or Apple (on iOS) under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, card or payment details. The store tells the game which product was bought; the game keeps that purchase record (including the store's purchase token) only on your device so it knows you own it. Restore purchases in Settings asks the store again.

Crash reports and feedback (Sentry)

When the game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix bugs. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the game's code, the game version, and your device model and operating system version. It does not contain your name, contacts, location, screenshots or log files, and the game is set not to send personal data (such as your IP address as a user identifier) with it.

In the game you can also send us a short feedback note. It is sent the same way, together with the game version. You may add your email address if you want an answer; this is optional and we use it only to reply to you. Please do not write other personal details in the note.

Crash reports and feedback notes are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically. Sentry stores them on our behalf as a data processor.

No analytics

The game contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own. We only see the general statistics that Google Play Console and App Store Connect show every developer (for example the number of installs).

Permissions

The game uses the internet only for ads, purchases and crash reports or feedback. Google's ads and billing libraries add the permissions they need (network state, advertising ID, billing, keeping the device awake while an ad plays). The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Why we may handle this information (legal basis)

  • Personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and crash reports: our legitimate interest in funding the game with optional ads and in keeping it working.
  • Purchases: to give you what you bought.
  • Feedback and your email address: your choice to contact us, so that we can answer.

Children

Hornfall is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK you have the right to ask for access to the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the game has no accounts, we can usually only find a feedback note if you tell us your email address or roughly when you sent it. Write to jibbgamestudio@gmail.com and we will answer within one month.

For information collected by Google for ads, you can also use Google's own controls (My Ad Center).

You can complain to a data protection authority. In Sweden this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY): www.imy.se.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date before the change reaches the game.
