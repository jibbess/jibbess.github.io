Tumla – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Tumla is a game for iPhone (App Store). This page explains what happens with information when you play it.

What we collect

We, the makers of Tumla, do not sell any information. The game has no accounts and no login, and we run no servers that receive data about you. The only things that reach us are crash reports, and notes you choose to send – with your e-mail address only if you choose to add it (see below).

Your progress

Your progress and your settings are saved in a small file on your iPhone. The game does not use iCloud or Game Center, so your progress is not copied to any account by the game. (Your iPhone's own device backup may include it, under Apple's terms.) Deleting the game deletes your progress. Nothing about your progress is sent to us, except the level number in a crash report or a note.

Crash reports and feedback (Sentry)

When the game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix bugs. A report holds technical details only: what went wrong in the game's code, the game version, which game and level you are on, and your device model and system version. It does not hold your name, contacts or location, no screenshots and no log files are attached, and we turn off sending your IP address as personal data. If you write to us with the Write to us button in the settings, your note is sent the same way together with the game name, version and level. Please don't write personal details in it.

If you would like an answer, you can also type your e-mail address in the box under your note. This is optional: leave it empty and no address is sent. If you give one, it is sent to Sentry with your note and we use it only to reply to you about that note – never for marketing, and it is never sold or shared. Reports and notes, including any address, are kept for up to 90 days and are used only to fix the game and answer you. To have your note and address deleted sooner, write to the contact address below. Sentry's privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy.

Video ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward (a hint). Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one. If you have bought Remove ads, the game shows no ads at all: you get the reward without a video.

These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect your device's IP address and the approximate location it gives, device identifiers, how you use the ads and the game, and crash and performance data. Only ads suitable for all ages are requested. The game requests non-personalised ads only, and it does not show Apple's tracking question (App Tracking Transparency), because it does not use your advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking.

Before any ad is requested, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button in the game's settings, shown where the form applies. If consent is required and you have not given it, the game shows no ads at all.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy Remove ads (once) and a pack of hints. We never see your card, Apple ID or payment details, and we keep no record of your purchases on any server. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple tells the game only which product was bought and a transaction number, so it can be given to you once; the game keeps that number in its save file on your iPhone. "Remove ads" can be restored on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID: it comes back by itself, or tap Restore purchases in the settings. Hints from a pack are used up when they are given, so they cannot be restored. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Permissions

The game uses the internet only for ads, purchases, crash reports and your feedback, and vibration for a short buzz in play (you can turn it off in the settings). The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it shows no tracking question.

Children

Tumla is made for everyone aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. The game tells Google that it is not child-directed and that the player is not under the age of consent, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Ads are limited to those suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change reaches the game.
